Project health check

Twenty-five questions to tell, in fifteen minutes, whether your building project is on track. Score each one, add up the total, and see where to act first.

How to score: Yes = 2 points, Partly = 1, No = 0. Answer from evidence you can show someone, not from what you have been told. Maximum 50.

What your score means

TotalStatusWhat to do
40 to 50HealthyKeep the reviews going; fix the few zeros.
25 to 39Needs attentionFix the zeros in Time and Cost first; they compound fastest.
Below 25At riskGet an independent review before more money is paid out.
Warning signs that override the score: payments running ahead of progress, the finish date moving every month, work being redone, or the contractor reducing labour on site.
