Independent by design

We don't build, supply or design. We manage, on behalf of the people paying for the building, and nobody else.

How we started

AVSPMC was set up in 2012 by engineers who had spent years working for contractors on Indian building sites. On project after project they saw the same thing: the owner was paying for everything but knew the least about what was happening.

So we set up a consultancy that works only for owners. Our project managers, quantity surveyors, planners and site engineers now manage residential, commercial and private projects across India, with one rule: the client hears bad news first, and hears it from us.

How we work

One side only

We take no commissions, referral fees or margins from contractors and suppliers. Our only income on your project is our fee.

Bad news early

A delay reported in week two is a decision. Reported in month six, it is a dispute. We tell you early, with the options and their costs.

Engineers on site

Our site teams are qualified civil and MEP engineers who check work in person, not from contractors' reports.

One page a week

Every client gets a weekly one-page status covering programme, cost, risks and the decisions needed. Detailed reports are there when you want them.
