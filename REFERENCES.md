# Reference sites: 10 leading PMC firms in India

No independent "top 10 PMC firms in India" ranking exists; the published lists are mostly paid
listicles. This is a selection of the firms most active in real estate and building PMC in India,
based on their scale, India track record and industry awards (researched November 2026). Figures
are what each firm states on its own site or in trade press.

## What the AVSPMC site takes from them

- **Track-record numbers near the top** (Colliers, JLL, ANP, TCE): the stats band under the hero.
- **Services as a scannable list with sub-items** (Colliers): services page, nine services, each hireable alone.
- **A page per sector** (TCE, Turner & Townsend, JLL): sectors page, narrowed to real estate and buildings.
- **Project cards with sector, city, area and cost** (ANP, Turner): project sheets, plus client type, services and status.
- **Filtering projects** (Turner, TCE): sector and status filters on the projects page.
- **WhatsApp as a first-class contact route** (ANP): WhatsApp in the footer, CTA band and contact form.
- **Enquiry form that asks for project type and stage** (Turner & Townsend, JLL): contact form.

## What it deliberately does differently

- Every reference site is navy/blue or dark green on white with stock photography. AVSPMC uses
  graphite, concrete greys and site-safety yellow, and draws its own building sketches, so it
  doesn't look like a smaller copy of a global firm.
- The big firms describe capabilities. AVSPMC leads with a position: *we work only on the owner's
  side*, which is what developers, corporates and private owners actually buy from a PMC.
- The homepage hero is a project programme (Gantt chart), the core working document of a PMC,
  instead of a stock photo with a slogan.
