Privacy policy

What we collect when you contact us through this website, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. Last updated 13 November 2026.

Who we are

This website is run by AVSPMC ("AVSPMC", "we", "us"), a project management consultancy for building projects in India. For any question about your personal data, write to .

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us through the enquiry form:

  • your name and phone number;
  • your email address, if you give it;
  • details of your project: type, city, approximate area, current stage and your message.

We don't ask for, and you shouldn't send, financial details, identity documents or anything about your health, religion or caste.

We measure visits with Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without identifying you. We don't use advertising trackers.

Why we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry, discuss your project and, if you become a client, to manage our work with you. We don't sell your data or share it for anyone else's marketing.

By sending the form, you agree to us using your details for these purposes. You can withdraw that consent at any time by writing to us; this won't affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

Where it is kept and who sees it

  • Storage: enquiries are stored in a database run by Supabase, on servers in Mumbai, India.
  • Notifications: we may receive a copy of each enquiry by email, sent through our email provider.
  • WhatsApp: if you choose "Send on WhatsApp", your message goes through WhatsApp under WhatsApp's own privacy policy.
  • Access: only AVSPMC staff who handle enquiries can see them, through a password-protected admin page.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries that don't lead to work for up to two years, then delete them. For clients, we keep project records for as long as the law and our contracts require.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct or complete it;
  • delete it, unless we have to keep it by law;
  • stop using it, by withdrawing your consent.

Write to with "Personal data" in the subject. We reply within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with our reply, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
